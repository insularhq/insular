# Names

Insular and Pharos are names used by VDHome (Belgium) for its open-source projects.
None of them is a registered trademark.

- You may use the names Insular and Pharos for unmodified builds of this repository, and to say that your work
  uses or is compatible with Insular.
- A modified build distributed to others must carry a different name, so that nobody mistakes it for this project.
  "Based on Insular" in its description is fine.
- The EUPL-1.2 licence of the code grants no rights in these names: its article 5 ("Legal Protection") does not
  permit use of the Licensor's trade names or trademarks beyond describing the origin of the Work.
