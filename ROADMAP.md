# Roadmap

Where Insular stands and where it goes next, with no promised dates. Issues and milestones on this repository will carry
the public work as it starts.

## Done
- The model: registers, generators, checkers, the policy bundle and the decision records.
- A GitOps tree for a size S site, tried in parts on a private lab.

## Next
- Prove a size S site from start to finish on a lab, with a security summary of what was checked.
- Federation between two organisations, played as an exercise.

## Then
- Documentation for adopters: what a small organisation needs to read and run.

## Later
- A second maintainer or custodian for releases and ceremonies.
